DAPComposer

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DAPComposer ("the Application"), operated by Major Talent Studios, LLC ("we," "us," or "our"), handles your information. We built DAPComposer to run entirely on your device, so there isn't much to disclose — but here are the details.

1. Data Collection

DAPComposer does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, telemetry, or analytics, and does not use third-party analytics services, crash reporters, or advertising networks.

The only information we receive is what you choose to submit to us directly, such as through our contact form. This may include your message, the DAPComposer version you're using, and any contact details you provide, and is used solely to respond to your inquiry.

2. Local File Processing

All audio scanning, file reading, and library writing happen strictly on the user's local file system. DAPComposer does not upload your music, playlists, or metadata to any server, and it does not require an internet connection to function.

3. Payments and In-App Purchases

Payments and in-app purchase transactions are processed entirely by Apple via the App Store, so you never touch or view billing data through DAPComposer, and we never receive or store it.

4. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, including through our contact form. If you believe a child has submitted information to us, please contact us so we can delete it.

5. Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy at any time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the Application following any updates signifies your acceptance of the revised policy.

6. Contact Information

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us.
