DAPComposer

Manage your local music library, and sync it straight to your digital audio player.

Features

Effortless Library Management

Point DAPComposer at your local music folders and it takes care of the rest, keeping your artists, albums, and playlists organized and ready to sync.

One-Click Sync

Select the artists, albums, or playlists you want, then sync them to your digital audio player in one step — no manual file copying required.

Playlist Creator

Create custom playlists by selecting songs from your library, then sync them to your digital audio player with a single click.

Rescan Library

Rescan your music library at any time to pick up new additions or changes to your collection, then sync to update your device — no more mismatches between your library and what's on your player.

FAQ

Does DAPComposer work with any digital audio player?

DAPComposer works with any device that mounts as a writable storage volume on macOS, including most third-party and open-source firmware players. It does not support stock Apple devices such as iPods or iPhones. It should work with iPods running Rockbox or other third-party firmware.

What files does DAPComposer work with?

DAPComposer works with most common audio file formats, including MP3, M4A, FLAC, and WAV. Also supported are cover images (e.g., Cover.jpg) and lyric (LRC) files. Files containing DRM (digital rights management) protection are not supported.

Does DAPComposer require an internet connection?

No. DAPComposer runs entirely on your Mac and does not require an internet connection to function. All library scanning, playlist creation, and syncing happens locally on your Mac.

My library scan is taking a long time. Why?

Library scans, particularly the initial scan, can take time depending on the size of your collection and the speed of your storage device. Subsequent scans are typically faster. Consider scanning during periods of low system activity.

Can I sync multiple libraries?

Yes and No. While DAPComposer does allow you to have multiple libraries, you can only sync one at a time.

Can I sync multiple devices?

Yes. DAPComposer allows you to add and sync multiple digital audio players. Each device can have its own selection of artists, albums, and playlists.

After I sync my files, I don't see them on my device. Why not?

Some devices may require rescanning the library on device or importing playlists manually.

Can I modify the files on my device?

This is not recommended. DAPComposer utilizes a one-way sync and may overwrite any changes made to files on your device during synchronization. Changes made directly on the device are not sent back to your Mac.

Does DAPComposer support streaming services?

No. DAPComposer is designed to manage and sync your local music library only. It does not support streaming services such as Spotify, Apple Music, or Tidal.

Is my music library uploaded anywhere?

No. All library scanning and syncing happens locally on your Mac — your files never leave your device.
